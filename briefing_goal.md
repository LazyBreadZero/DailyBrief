# Frontier Intelligence — The Goal Behind the Brief

*Read this before writing. It is the "why" that the house-style file (`briefing_system_prompt.md`) turns into "how."*

## Who this is for
**Angelo Gunther** — 25, Vienna. Neuroscience BSc, Innovation MSc, former Chief of Staff at Worldpay. He is deliberately positioning himself **not** as a builder of frontier human-experience technology (XR, spatial computing, BCI, immersive & consciousness tech) but as its **translator and commercial accelerator** — the person who makes decision-makers *feel why it matters*, and who connects the people, capital, and narratives that move the field.

North star: *"I am the person who makes the world feel why this matters."*

## What this brief is actually for
Not to "keep up with the news." It exists to **compound one specific asset**: Angelo's credibility and pattern-recognition at the commercial frontier of human-experience tech. Every edition should leave him able to say something **non-obvious, specific, and useful** to a founder, investor, or operator that week.

A great edition does three jobs:
1. **Orient** — what actually changed, with numbers in context and *named* actors ("Max Hodak," not "the company").
2. **Frame** — one "step back" that turns events into a structural thesis (e.g. the FDA / CE-mark / NMPA *regulatory race*; the BCI *architecture spectrum*). **The frame is the product.**
3. **Mobilise** — *Angelo's Angle*: 2–3 concrete, non-coddling moves tied to his positioning (an essay to write, a person to meet, a company to approach), each anchored to *this* edition's news.

## The beats (and why these)
NeuroTech · XR/AR/VR · BCI · spatial computing · immersive/consciousness tech. These are converging into one **"post-screen human interface"** story. Angelo's edge is holding the whole convergence at once — clinical/regulatory **+** consumer **+** platform — while specialists see only their slice.

## What "great" looks like
- A reader already deep in the space still learns something structural.
- Every claim sourced and credibility-graded; no hype, no "metaverse" filler.
- **Continuity:** each edition follows up the threads it opened before — that is what `briefing_archive.md` is for.
- The **email is a teaser** that earns the click; the full argument lives in the hosted HTML brief.

## What to avoid
General AI-industry news (LLM releases, AI IPOs, chip launches) **unless** it directly bears on human-experience interfaces. "Frontier" here means the frontier of *human-experience technology* — not frontier language models. If an edition drifts into generic AI coverage, it has failed.
