Prosus Leads a €480m Round in Alan, Valuing the French Health Insurer at €5.5 Billion

Paris-based Alan said on June 24th that it had raised €480m in a Series G round led by Prosus, with existing backers Index Ventures and Teachers’ Venture Growth returning and Dara Holdings joining as a new investor. The round values the health insurer at €5.5bn, up from the €5bn it reached in March. Prosus (9/10) Sifted (8/10)

Alan posted more than €800m in annualised recurring revenue in the first quarter of 2026, up 53% year on year, serving 1.1 million members and 37,000 businesses across France, Spain, Belgium and Canada. It is profitable in France, its largest market, on a team of 850 people. PR Newswire (7/10)

Fahd Beg, Prosus’s head of investments, called healthcare “one of the most significant global opportunities for AI-led transformation,” the same language Prosus has used moving into health after building positions in classifieds and fintech. Alan’s own pitch is structural rather than incremental: insurance, prevention and care delivery sold and run as one platform, not three products stitched together. CEO Jean-Charles Samuelian-Werve said the capital buys the means to “expand to new countries, deepen our presence where we already are, invest even more in AI and product.”

Julinka’s Angle
1 Alan's €800m ARR and 53% growth is now the live comp for what "prevention sold as the core product, not a wellness feature" looks like at scale. Worth benchmarking any preventive-health company in Calm/Storm's pipeline on ARR-per-member and growth rate against Alan, not against an earlier-stage peer set — the gap will be instructive either way.
2 Beg's framing was AI-led transformation of healthcare, not AI bolted onto an existing platform. Worth asking each preventive-health company already in diligence whether its AI roadmap survives being asked the same question, or whether AI is doing marketing work rather than product work.
