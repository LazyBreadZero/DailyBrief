Ingredion Bought a Carrot Extract Before Brussels Finished Approving It

On 16 June the European Commission authorised Benicaros — a prebiotic fibre that NutriLeads makes by extracting Rhamnogalacturonan-I from carrot pomace left over from juicing — as a novel food under Regulation (EU) 2026/1306. The clearance permits up to 1,500mg a day in supplements for anyone over three, plus broader use across packaged food, and grants whoever holds the dossier five years of exclusive commercial rights to the ingredient across the EU, running to July 2031. NutraIngredients (8/10)

The dossier-holder, as of 2 June, is Ingredion. The US ingredients giant announced that day it had acquired Benicaros from NutriLeads in an asset deal — the patent, the trademark, the human clinical-trial data, the manufacturing know-how — financial terms undisclosed. Ingredion signed two weeks before Brussels finished the paperwork that turns a Dutch biotech’s ingredient into a five-year exclusive. GlobeNewswire / Ingredion (10/10)

EFSA's underlying safety opinion on the ingredient predates the acquisition by months; what Ingredion priced on 2 June was the probability that a scientific opinion already on file would convert into a legal authorisation, not the science itself. It paid for the gap between an EFSA finding and a Commission signature — the rigour, not the result — and the gap closed in its favour fourteen days later.

Also Today · Art

The Louvre Says It Is Running Out of Steam

Two days after Hauser & Wirth sold a Picasso for $35 million on Art Basel's VIP day, Christophe Leribault, the Louvre's director since February, told a French Senate committee that the world's most-visited museum is “running out of steam.” Its building systems are at the end of their working life, he said: “Building-related emergencies are piling up, and we're facing a wall in terms of investments.” The museum is still absorbing a roughly €10 million ticket-fraud scheme and the fallout from October's $100 million jewellery heist, which exposed security gaps Leribault inherited along with the job. France 24 (9/10)

The same week the private art market wrote nine-figure cheques without hesitation, the institution that draws nine million visitors a year told lawmakers it cannot fund its own roof.
