Indonesia Will Issue 30 Million Carbon Credits on 6 July — the Largest Government Forestry Supply Since the 2022 Export Ban

Speaking at the Net Zero Delivery Summit in London this week — part of London Climate Action Week — Indonesia’s forestry minister Raja Juli Antoni announced he will sign a ministerial approval facilitating the issuance of more than 30 million tonnes of CO₂e in REDD+ forestry credits on 6 July. Three days later, on 9 July, Indonesia will launch its Carbon Unit Registry System (SRUK), a national infrastructure designed to record carbon transactions, improve traceability, and address the transparency failures that led the government to ban credit exports in 2022. Argus Media (9/10) Carbon Pulse (9/10)

The scale matters. The voluntary carbon market has been waiting for Indonesian forestry supply since the government lifted the export ban at the end of 2025. More than 30 million tonnes of CO₂e in a single government issuance is among the largest single-jurisdiction releases on record. The Katingan Mentaya REDD+ project — 149,800 hectares of peatland in Kalimantan, one of the largest tropical-forest protection projects in the world — was among the projects suspended during the 2022 moratorium.

The SRUK registry is the structural question. A government-controlled registry is Indonesia’s bid to internalise the audit function that Verra and Gold Standard have held by default in the voluntary market. Whether a sovereign registry confers credibility or concentrates political risk in a single institution is the transparency argument the market will price in real time once credits start moving.

Art · Heritage Finance

The Louvre’s infrastructure crisis is now publicly named and institutionally visible. A burst heating pipe above the Egyptian antiquities library drenched hundreds of documents — some from the late 19th century — in late 2025; in subsequent months, director Laurence des Cars told France’s Senate the museum’s buildings were “no longer watertight” and “running out of steam.” Workers at the CGT, CFDT, and Sud unions voted unanimously to begin a rolling strike. A major overhaul of the heating and ventilation system is scheduled to start in September 2026. No public capital commitment for the broader structural programme has been announced. artnet News (9/10)

Food · Cultivated Protein

The competing-bid deadline for UPSIDE Foods’ $50 million stalking-horse offer on Believer Meats’ North Carolina production facility is July 20; if competing bids arrive, the auction is July 28, with a court hearing July 30. The minimum qualifying bid is $52.25 million. Believer’s facility was valued at $600 million when the company last raised capital. UPSIDE’s offer — 87.5% below that valuation — is the market’s current mark for what an idle cultivated-meat production plant is worth. AgFunderNews (9/10)
