The Lewis Collection Sells for $392.6 Million, the Biggest Single-Owner Auction Ever Held in Europe

Sotheby’s sold the 50-work art collection of the British billionaire Joe Lewis in London on 24 June for £296.3m ($392.6m), the highest total any single-owner sale has ever achieved in Europe. The house had estimated the collection at £150m–£200m going in. Artnet News (9/10)

The top lot was Amedeo Modigliani’s Nu assis au collier (1917–18), which sold for $63.9m, a new European auction record for the artist. Lewis had bought the painting at Christie’s in 1995 for $12.4m — better than a fivefold gain held privately for three decades before reaching the market again this week. France 24 (9/10)

René Magritte’s La Belle Promenade, a gouache unseen in public for nearly 60 years, sold for £16m ($21.2m) — four times its high estimate and a new record for a work on paper by the artist. ARTnews (9/10)

The sale lands in the middle of London’s June fair week — Treasure House Fair at the Royal Hospital Chelsea, Classic Art London across Mayfair, and Studiolo, a one-evening new fair staged in Westminster Abbey’s College Garden around a newly rediscovered Valentin van Orley panel estimated at £1.5m. Against that backdrop of modest, dealer-led trade, the Lewis result reads as confirmation that the very top of the market is pricing scarcity and provenance, not breadth. The Art Newspaper (9/10)

A Bristol Fusion Startup Raises £23m to Manufacture Medical Isotopes

Astral Systems, a Bristol company running multi-state fusion reactors out of the former Berkeley Power Station, closed a £23m first close this week, taking its total funding past £28m. Mercia Ventures led, joined by Tees River, Daphni and Blast Club alongside existing backers Speedinvest and Playfair. The company already operates three commercial fusion facilities and has booked more than £3m in research revenue; it is using the round to begin producing medical radioisotopes — used in cancer diagnosis and treatment, and currently in chronically fragile global supply — with first production targeted for early 2027. Tech.eu (9/10)
