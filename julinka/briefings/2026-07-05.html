Formo’s Precision-Fermented Casein Enters Active FDA Review; US Commercial Debut Is in Eight Days

Formo, the Berlin-based precision fermentation company, has confirmed that its recombinant casein — co-developed with Belgian biotech Those Vegan Cowboys — is now under active FDA GRAS review, making it the first precision-fermented casein to reach this stage in the United States. The company expects a no-objection letter later in 2026. Formo has simultaneously launched a US subsidiary, Formo Foods Inc., appointed Rx Food Ingredients LLC as its US sales and go-to-market partner, and is already sampling with US food and nutrition customers ahead of a commercial debut at IFT FIRST in Chicago on 13–16 July. Vegconomist 8/10 Green Queen 7/10

Casein is where precision fermentation faces its hardest functional test in dairy. It is the dominant structural protein in cheese: its micelle behaviour governs texture, melt, and stretch in ways that plant-protein alternatives have consistently failed to replicate at commercial quality. Formo’s recombinant casein is genetically identical to bovine casein but produced in microbial hosts without livestock. The FDA GRAS self-determination pathway allows Formo to file its own safety dossier; active review status means FDA has taken it up rather than leaving it in queue. If no-objection is issued, the company can commercialise in the US without waiting for formal agency approval — a materially faster route than European competitors face under the EU novel-food regulation, which the first Biotech Act of December 2025 explicitly left out of its new regulatory sandbox. Food Ingredients First 8/10

IFT FIRST is the US food industry’s largest annual trade event. Formo’s decision to debut its casein applications there — rather than at a European venue — is deliberate: the US is its first commercial market, and the IFT FIRST audience (food scientists, ingredient buyers, R&D leads at major manufacturers) is precisely the customer base for functional dairy-protein supply. The company already holds €35 million in EIB debt financing for production expansion. The casein pipeline opens applications across hard cheese analogues, processed cheese formats, and high-protein nutrition — categories where functional parity with bovine casein, at competitive cost, has been the missing link. EIB 10/10

Further reporting

Indonesia’s 30-million-tonne REDD+ tranche issues tomorrow. Indonesia will release more than 30 million tonnes of CO2e in forestry credits on 6 July — the country’s largest-ever REDD+ issuance — with the Verra agreement and four internationally-approved projects in place and the national Carbon Unit Registry System (SRUK) launching on 9 July. Forestry minister Raja Juli Antoni confirmed the timeline at London Climate Action Week. Katingan Mentaya spot prices have declined on anticipated supply volume; the first week of SRUK operations will set the reference price for sovereign-registry REDD+ credits for the rest of 2026. Julinka’s Angle
1 Formo will be at IFT FIRST Chicago in eight days demonstrating prototype applications of its casein across cheese and nutrition categories. This is the first public commercial showing of precision-fermented casein in the US, and Rx Food Ingredients LLC is handling US customer conversations now. Request a meeting with the Formo team before July 13 — either through Rx Food Ingredients or directly via Formo’s LinkedIn — to see which application categories are commercially ready versus still in development. If Calm/Storm is building a thesis on precision fermentation as a scalable dairy-protein platform, this is the moment to put a number on the functional gap the casein closes and who would buy it first.
2 Monday’s Indonesia REDD+ issuance is the first real-world test of whether a sovereign national registry (SRUK) adds credibility premium to voluntary carbon prices or exposes political concentration risk. Watch the Katingan Mentaya price movement through 9 July. If prices recover on issuance, that is the market pricing sovereign governance as additive. If they continue to slide, the 30-million-tonne volume is simply overwhelming demand at current quality expectations. Either outcome tells you something concrete about where nature-credit buyers are placing their trust threshold — directly relevant to any LP conversation Calm/Storm is having on biodiversity finance.
