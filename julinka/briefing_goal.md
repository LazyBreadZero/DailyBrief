# Appetite — The Goal Behind the Brief

*Read this before writing. It is the "why" that the house-style file (`julinka/briefing_system_prompt.md`) turns into "how."*

## Who this is for
**Julinka Naomi Waesche** — ETH Zurich-trained food scientist (research on yeast in grape must), three years a senior food consultant at **Migros**, **Baby VC** alumna, now joining **Calm/Storm Ventures** (Vienna early-stage health/impact fund, expanding into nutrition & climate tech). She reads the *FT* and the *Economist* at breakfast; she loves **art** (Bauhaus, botanical painting) and the **natural world** (vineyards, mountains, climbing, cross-country skiing). Her own line: *"Curiosity, innovation, food, and a healthy appetite for projects with range."*

## What this brief is actually for
To **compound her rarest asset — range with rigour.** Julinka is the unusual investor who can read the *science* **and** the *market* **and** the *culture*. The brief exists to make that edge sharper and more visible every day: to help her source and diligence better than a generalist, and to build a public identity as **"the food scientist who became a venture investor."**

The throughline, stated once and meant: **prevention is the category, nutrition is the substrate, rigour is the moat.**

A great edition does three jobs:
1. **Orient** across her four worlds, with numbers in context and named actors.
2. **Bridge** — find where the pillars meet (agrifoodtech VC, nature finance, food-as-culture-as-capital). **The bridges are her vantage point** — nobody else stands exactly there.
3. **Mobilise** — *Julinka's Angle*: 2–3 concrete moves tied to her new role (a sourcing thesis to write, a person to meet at Art Basel, a claim to publish), anchored to *this* edition's news.

## The four pillars (and why these)
- **Venture Capital** — her new craft; especially European early-stage health / impact / nutrition (Calm/Storm's turf).
- **Food science & trends** — her deepest expertise: meal replacement, fermentation, alternative & cultivated protein, food regulation, and cultural waves (e.g. K-food / hallyu).
- **Art** — genuine fluency (Bauhaus, botanical); also where Swiss capital and culture meet (Art Basel, on her doorstep).
- **Nature** — her natural habitat *and* an emerging asset class (biodiversity / nature credits, regenerative agriculture) that bridges to the fund's climate mandate.

## What "great" looks like
- She finishes an edition with something she can **use in a meeting that week**.
- *FT/Economist* register: declarative, numerate, allergic to hype.
- Continuity via `julinka/briefing_archive.md`; the **email teases**, the hosted HTML brief delivers.

## What to avoid
Generic "food news" with no science or capital angle; pretty art coverage with no thesis. Always tie back to the throughline.
