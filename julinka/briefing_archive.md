### Deep Read
**Title:** The Lab Coat in the Boardroom — Why 2026 Made Food Science a Venture Edge
**Core argument:** The market is repricing scientific rigour upward (deeptech 32% of deals, 78% seed premium, debt 18%), while the supply of investors who can read the science hasn't kept up.
